#Cost-Effectiveness Model
##Overview

This project presents a synthetic health economics demonstration of a cost-effectiveness model comparing two hypothetical treatment strategies for a chronic condition.

The project is designed for portfolio purposes and does not contain original coursework, assignment questions, assignment answers, confidential data, marked academic work, supervisor feedback or publication-ready results. All inputs are illustrative and simplified.

##Objective

To demonstrate how a cost-effectiveness model can estimate costs, health outcomes and incremental cost-effectiveness between two treatment options.

##Intervention and Comparator
Treatment A: Standard care
Treatment B: New intervention

The model compares expected costs and health outcomes associated with each treatment strategy over a defined time horizon.

##Model Type

A simplified cohort Markov model is used to simulate movement between health states over repeated cycles.

##Example Health States

The demonstration model includes simplified health states such as:

Stable disease
Progressed disease
Severe disease
Death
