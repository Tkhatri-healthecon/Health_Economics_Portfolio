# Interpreting Survival Curves and Hazard Ratios

## Overview
This project explains how survival curves and hazard ratios are interpreted in clinical trials and HTA decision-making.

## Topics Covered
- Kaplan–Meier survival curves
- Hazard ratios
- Confidence intervals
- Censoring in survival analysis
- Clinical interpretation and uncertainty

## Why This Matters
Survival evidence is frequently used to assess treatment effectiveness and reimbursement decisions in oncology and other disease areas. Understanding uncertainty and interpretation is essential in HTA.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Survival analysis interpretation
- Statistical literacy
- Evidence interpretation
- Scientific communication
