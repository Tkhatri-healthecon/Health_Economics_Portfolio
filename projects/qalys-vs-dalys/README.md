# QALYs vs DALYs in Healthcare Decision-Making

## Overview
This project compares quality-adjusted life years (QALYs) and disability-adjusted life years (DALYs), two commonly used measures in health economics and public health.

## Topics Covered
- What QALYs measure
- What DALYs measure
- Key methodological differences
- Applications in HTA and global health
- Limitations and interpretation challenges

## Why This Matters
QALYs and DALYs are widely used to support healthcare prioritisation and resource allocation decisions. Understanding their differences is important when interpreting economic evaluations and population health analyses.
